The lists below are every Glyphed Rune Word and Spectral Parchment spell for Cleric, Druid, Shaman, Enchanter, and Bard, and the odds of each turn-in. Mark the spells the raid needs. The next class is the one with the most of those marks still short.

Each marked spell is collected until it has 2 copies. Officers can raise a spell as high as 9.

Those scrolls are DKPed at the start of the next Planes of Power raid night. Vex Thal Wednesday is not a spell DKP night.

When an item's marked spells all have their copies, DKP that item.

Either item can be switched to equal distribution. The next turn-in of that item goes to the class, among every class with that item's pool, that has the fewest turn-ins of it.

Parchments can be left untracked. Officers record how many Spectral Parchments were seen that night. Those parchments are DKPed.
