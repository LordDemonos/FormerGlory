How foci work

Except for range and spell haste, foci roll a random amount from 1% up to the listed max. That roll happens after skills and AAs. Complete Heals for Cleric, Druid, and Shaman are excluded from every focus except range.

  • Mana preservation is 1% to the listed max, after specialization and SCM. Example: GSS costs 540. SCM3 plus 200 evocation specialization save 21%, down to 426. Wind of Mana then rolls 1–20% on that remainder, so a full 20% proc lands at 341.
  • Spell haste is a fixed cut. Hybrids get 3% per level over 50. Every haste source shares a hard cap of 50% of the original cast time, including foci, AAs, and Blessing of the Faithful. See AA Issues before buying redundant haste AAs.
