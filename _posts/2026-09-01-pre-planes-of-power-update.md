## The Return of Saturday Raiding
Starting Saturday 8/29 we'll be earning DKP for raids again. I'm hoping since summer is about over, attendance will rise a little. We'll only be doing 2 raid ticks though. The idea is to give everyone an early night, hopefully burn through our targets, and get people back into the groove of Saturday raids before they fully return to 3 ticks in October. 
- Cursed Cycle starting at 9PM ET.
- If we have another warrior in need, I'd like a rogue to check Xerkizh The Creator and see if he's holding a Hategiver
- We'll move to Sleeper's Tomb after. Loot will be /random unless you voice you're willing to spend more than 10DKP for something, then it goes up for bid. Tradeable items will be DKP greed bids.
- If class comp sucks we'll pivot to Inner AC or Ring of Fear or somethin.

## What's happening in October?
I plan to make another post in the coming weeks, but right now the plan is to fully return to 4 nights of raiding starting October 1st. Friday Oct 2nd and Saturday Oct 3rd will be kind of up in the air. Honestly the first few weeks will be a lot like Luclin where things move around and we'll be gauging the value of targets as well as our clear speed. We'll do our best to communicate where to meet ahead of time, but everything is pretty much one zone away from Plane of Tranquility anyways. I do plan on moving Vex Thal to Wednesdays (First being October 7th), a day its never been on, and likely keeping it there till December 30th. Vex Thal loot will remain as DKP bids. I want to give people who can't always attend Mon/Fri a chance at the loot and the neck item. We'll do our best to clear the whole thing in one night.
