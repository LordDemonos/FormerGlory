Hey all, I've been putting a lot of time into [formerglory.lol](https://formerglory.lol) over the last couple weeks so we're not scrambling on October 1st. Same as Luclin, this is our space. ## Boss Guides and Flagging

[Strategy](https://formerglory.lol/strategy) now has Planes of Power. Zone event guides, individual boss pages, copy boxes for raid briefs, and a [flagging / progression guide](https://formerglory.lol/strategy/pop_progression) so you can see what order to do things and which hails you still need. Stats are from PQDI. If something looks wrong, tell me.

## Raid Loot

There's a new [Raid Loot](https://formerglory.lol/raid-loot) page covering Classic through PoP. You can search by item, boss, or zone, filter by class, and collapse the expansions you don't care about. Each boss links back to its strategy page. Hover the item names for the usual tooltips.

If an item is marked Retired, that's something that used to drop and doesn't anymore. Hide retired is on by default so you're not looking at junk from old tables.

## Spell Focus Items

[Spell Focus](https://formerglory.lol/focus) lets you filter by class, slot, and what the focus actually does.

## Alternate Advancement

[AAs](https://formerglory.lol/aa) can be searched and filtered by class. Click the `/alt` number to copy the activate command. 

## Raid Triggers

PoP packs are up for GINA, EQLogParser, and PQ Companion.

- [GINA](https://formerglory.lol/gina) — download `pop.gtp`
- [EQLogParser](https://formerglory.lol/eqlp) — download `pop.tgf.gz`
- [PQ Companion](https://formerglory.lol/pq-companion) — download `pop-triggers.json`

Fabio's Luclin pack is still there for anyone who never grabbed it.

If you want something else on the site, or you catch a bad number on a boss page, just let me know.
