Find **Etumer** in the southeast gargoyle city in [Plane of Nightmare](/strategy/hedge_maze). Hail, **brother**, **perhaps**. Group leaders say **I am ready**. **24** players. Waves start **5 minutes** after the first port. Kill adds before they reach the stage. After **36** adds, he goes live. Loot includes **Recurved Wormwood Bow** (**10%**).

He is **Slowable**. **Always Calls for Help**. Hits 843. 400k HP.

**Instill Nightmare** is a cast (Single, Fear up to level 52, Magic -300, 24s recast). **Devouring Nightmare** is a proc (PBAE, 150 DD, Unresistable, 24s recast).
