End of the [Plane of Torment](/strategy/keeper_of_sorrows) mini-dungeon after **[Maareq the Prophet](/strategy/maareq_the_prophet)**.

He is **Unslowable**. AE Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 973. 100k HP.

![The-Keeper-of-Sorrows-2](/assets/img/The-Keeper-of-Sorrows-2.jpg)

On death, hail **Tylis Newleaf**. He lasts 10 minutes and can give up to 72 flags. Hail first. Then one person says **ready to return** to port the raid back to the cage room.
