Far left staircase in [Tower of Solusek Ro](/strategy/tower_of_solusek_ro). Pull **a conjured automoton** (103.6k) out of the room first.

Keep the fight in his room. If he is pulled past the hallway he ports home, wipes hate, and heals. He emotes *You shall not distract me from my conjurings!*

![Xuzl-2](/assets/img/Xuzl-2.jpg)

He is **Slowable**. Dual Wield. Enrage. Hits 1030. Attack delay 12. 600k HP.

**Wrath of Xuzl** summons animated swords (48s recast). Kill the pets.

On death a **flaming cauldron** spawns. Click it. Three **a warder of Xuzl** spawn on the path. Be in the room before the kill.
