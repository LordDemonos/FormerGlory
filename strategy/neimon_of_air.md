Air trial. [Plane of Time](/strategy/plane_of_time). 18 people. 1 hour for all five trials.

Wave 1: four **An Air Phoenix Noble**. Root, mez, or neither. It changes each run.

Wave 2: four **Servitor of Xegony** and **Neimon of Air** on the back wall. Inner two mezzable. Outer two root or offtank. Kill Neimon first. Then the rooted birds. Then the mezzed birds.

He is **Slowable** (75% mit). AE Rampage. Flurry. Dual Wield. Hits 1150. Attack delay 17. 200k HP.

Dispel **Caustic Aura** (self, 125 DS).

**Caustic Atmosphere** is a PBAE (550 DD + 25% slow + 25% spell haste, Poison -150, 3 min recast).
