Go around the arena, up the stairs, and clear both halls.

Kill **Decorin Berik** (69.4k HP, hits 831) and **Decorin Grunhork** (69.6k HP, hits 831). Both Dual Wield and Enrage. They respawn in 5 minutes. Both must be dead at the same time. Do not kill them on the twin spawn points.

Event **Tallon Zek** and **Vallon Zek** spawn in the north and south halls, then roam toward Fake Rallos. Pull them apart. Kill both.

Event Tallon is **Unslowable**. Dual Wield. Hits 955. 440k HP. Same Barbs, Tallon's Balance, Avatar Power, and shadowstep as the south-wing Tallon. Levitate.

Event Vallon is **Slowable**. Flurry. Dual Wield. Hits 1053. 225k HP. **Vallon's Precision**. He splits. Kill the real Vallon. Offtank the add.

**Rallos Zek** (Fake) is **Slowable** (75% mit). AE Rampage. Dual Wield. Enrage. Hits 1072. Attack delay 11. 750k HP. **Sedition** is a proc (AC -442, Unresistable). Tank him in a corner. Melee at max range. He despawns in the 50s and the Warlord spawns in the arena.

Levitate down. Corner tank. Melee at max range for AE Rampage. A group kites the adds. Adds spawn about every minute. Adds do not despawn when he dies. Kill them after.

He is **Slowable** (75% mit). AE Rampage. Dual Wield. Enrage. Corpse Camper. Hits 1072. Attack delay 11. 1.05M HP.

**Rage of Zek** is a PBAE (400/tick + 50 mana/tick, Fire -100, AoE 200, 35s recast). **Sedition** is a proc (AC -442, Unresistable).

Hail the Planar Projection in the arena. It only answers the killer's raid or group, lasts 10 minutes, and can give up to 72 flags. You need Maelin after both [Tallon Zek](/strategy/tallon_zek) and [Vallon Zek](/strategy/vallon_zek) for that hail. Then hail **Grand Librarian Maelin** in Plane of Knowledge.
