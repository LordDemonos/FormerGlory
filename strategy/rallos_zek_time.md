Phase 5. [Plane of Time](/strategy/plane_of_time). Defensive on the tank.

He is **Unslowable**. Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 2200. Attack delay 9. 1.7M HP.

Adds at **90%** (4), **75%** (4), **50%** (4), **25%** (6). Kill them as they spawn.

**Rage of Zek** is a PBAE (400/tick + 50 mana/tick, Fire -100, 9 curse, 35s recast). Cure curse.

**Vindictive Strike** is a single (3500 DD + 20s stun, Magic -200, 3 min recast). Random raid member.

**Blind Rage** is a self buff (65% haste + ATK 250 + AC -177, Unresistable, 7 min recast).
