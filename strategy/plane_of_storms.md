Outdoor nameds and camp Lords. Askr's Bastion flag is on [Bastion of Thunder](/strategy/bastion_of_thunder).

The three Lords respawn in **6 hours**. Secondary medallion minibosses in **18 hours**. Matching trash has a **20%** medallion chance. Trash respawns in **19 minutes 30 seconds**.

## Camps

Each Lord activates when that camp's required giants are dead. Matching trash has a **20%** medallion chance. Secondary medallion minibosses drop **1–3** Esoteric Medallions. **Gurebk**, **Jeplak**, and **Neffiken** drop **5–8**.

- **Srerendi** — south water. **[Faruek the Bold](/strategy/faruek_the_bold)**, **[Paruek the Strong](/strategy/paruek_the_strong)**, then **[Jeplak, Lord of Srerendi](/strategy/jeplak_lord_of_srerendi)**
- **Kelek\`Vor** — northeast forest. **[Laruken the Rigid](/strategy/laruken_the_rigid)**, **[Zertuken the Unyielding](/strategy/zertuken_the_unyielding)**, then **[Neffiken, Lord of Kelek\`Vor](/strategy/neffiken_lord_of_kelek_vor)**
- **Krendic** — west desert. **[Pendubk the Turbulent](/strategy/pendubk_the_turbulent)**, **[Solnebk the Unruly](/strategy/solnebk_the_unruly)**, then **[Gurebk, Lord of Krendic](/strategy/gurebk_lord_of_krendic)**

Minis are **Unslowable**. Flurry. Double Attack. Rampage. Hits 984. 177k HP.

**[Faruek the Bold](/strategy/faruek_the_bold)** (177k, hits 984). Rampage. Flurry. **Unslowable**. **Always Calls for Help**. Storm adds every **60 seconds**.

## Jeplak, Lord of Srerendi

South. Clear every water giant. He is **Unslowable**. Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 1246. 177k HP. Disease immune. **Wind Strike** is a proc (75 DD + FD, Magic 0, 12s recast).

Water giants can FD the tank. Adds spawn when someone dies.

## Neffiken, Lord of Kelek`Vor

Northeast. Clear every forest giant. He is **Unslowable**. Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 1481. 305k HP. Cold immune.

Treants spawn when he takes damage. Split them or kill them. **Manabolt** is a proc (1500 mana + 100 stam, Unresistable, 18s recast).

## Gurebk, Lord of Krendic

West. Clear every desert giant. He is **Unslowable**. Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 1481. 305k HP. **Stone Gale** is a proc (Targeted AE, 1500 DD + 2s stun, Unresistable).

Two scorpions on pull. Split them. More adds during the fight.

## Ston`Ruak, Ancient of the Trees

Kill every stormrider in the forest. Eight elders and priests spawn. Kill those. **[Ston`Ruak](/strategy/ston_ruak_ancient_of_the_trees)** spawns.

He is **Unslowable**. Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 1481. 305k HP.

Eight **an earthen spirit** on engage. Mezzable. They respawn while he is up. Lock the adds, kill him, then the spirits.

## Falto, Lord of Thunder

Hail **Relv the Mysterious** in the northwest forest. Follow his dialog through **we are courageous enough to go forth**. Group leaders say **we are ready** to port in.

Kill the mephits. Each death splits into three. When they are dead, **[Falto, Lord of Thunder](/strategy/falto_lord_of_thunder)** spawns. **3 hours**. He summons adds. Those adds do not split.

He is **Unslowable**. Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 1137. 305k HP. Fire immune. **Maelstrom of Thunder** is a proc (Targeted AE, 900 DD, Magic 0, 12s recast).

After loot, say **I am ready** at the fallen treant.

## Drornok Tok Vo`Lok

Kill every frog in the center. He spawns. Hits 878. 175k HP. **Unslowable**. Rampage. Frog adds every **2 minutes**.
