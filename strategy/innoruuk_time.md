Phase 5. [Plane of Time](/strategy/plane_of_time). Knight with fear immunity on tank. Healers can heal through the wall from the Quarm click room.

He is **Unslowable**. Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 2106. Attack delay 11. 1.7M HP.

Adds at **80%** (3) and **20%** (4). Kill them or root them. Slow the adds.

Dispel **Barrier of Hatred** (self, 225 DS + 1800 absorb). Melee will pincushion it.

**Seething Hatred** is a PBAE (550/tick + 75% agro + fear, Fire -200, 4 min recast).
