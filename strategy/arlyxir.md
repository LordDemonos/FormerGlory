Second staircase from the left in [Tower of Solusek Ro](/strategy/tower_of_solusek_ro). Clear the room first.

Keep the fight in her room. If she is pulled toward the hallway she ports home, wipes hate, and heals.

She is **Slowable**. Dual Wield. Enrage. Hits 1030. Attack delay 16. 750k HP. She is mezzable.

**Unity of Fire** is a self buff (35 DS + 35 FR). Dispel it.

At **12.5 minutes** she emotes *is immolated in flames, and is reborn!*, full heals, and casts **Searing Flames** (Targeted AE, FR -20). Kill her before then.

On death a **flaming cauldron** spawns. Click it. Three **a warder of Arlyxir** spawn on the path. Be in the room before the kill.
