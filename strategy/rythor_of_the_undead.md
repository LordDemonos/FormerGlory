Undead trial. [Plane of Time](/strategy/plane_of_time). 18 people. 1 hour for all five trials. Four waves. Trash is **Slowable**.

Wave 1: three **an undead guardian**. Unmezzable. Rootable.

Wave 2: four **an undead guardian**. Unmezzable. Rootable.

Wave 3: four **an undead guardian**. Mezzable.

Wave 4: two **an undead protector** and **Rythor of the Undead**.

He is **Slowable** (75% mit). AE Rampage. Flurry. Dual Wield. Hits 1150. Attack delay 17. 250k HP.

**Insidious Calamity** is a PBAE (575 DD + 350/tick + 50% mana cost, Cold, 3 min recast).

**Insipid Decay** is a single (1000 DD + 5s stun, Disease -150, 5 min recast).
