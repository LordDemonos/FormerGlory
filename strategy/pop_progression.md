## Flags

Sit at **Seer Mal Nae`Shi** in Plane of Knowledge, in the building next to the Plane of Tranquility stone. Say **guided meditation** for your current flags. Say **unlock my memories** to put them in order. Repeat both until nothing new.

**Grand Librarian Maelin** is at the top of the Plane of Knowledge library elevator. He gives four flags. He does not put them in order. Hail him, then say **what lore** and **what information**. Repeat until nothing new.

- After [Saryrn](/strategy/saryrn) and [Lord Mithaniel Marr](/strategy/lord_mithaniel_marr).
- After Marr and [Agnarr the Storm Lord](/strategy/agnarr_the_storm_lord). Say **what lore**.
- After that Saryrn and Marr flag, and both [Tallon Zek](/strategy/tallon_zek) and [Vallon Zek](/strategy/vallon_zek). Say **what lore**.
- After the Agnarr flag and [Rallos Zek the Warlord](/strategy/rallos_zek_the_warlord). Say **what information**.

If Tallon, Vallon, and Rallos die in one raid, hail Maelin before the Seer. Rallos gives a checklist flag until Maelin has the Zek flag.

If an elemental stone or a Planar Projection refuses you, bounce Seer and Maelin until neither gives more. If you see a checklist flag instead of a character flag, bounce them the same way.

Planar Projections answer the killer's raid or group only. Hail inside the timer. Caps are on the boss pages.

One unflagged can piggy per five flagged.

**One person** keys open doors for the raid. Everyone still needs the character flags and the Projection hails.

## Raid order

PoP zones need **level 46**. `#popflags` shows your progression flags: overview, **1** through **5**, **time**, or **all**. `#timelockout` shows your Plane of Time timeline. `#timelockout 1` through `#timelockout 6` show one phase. `#glory` shows your Rallosian Glory rank, title, and bonuses. `#petstats` shows your pet's HP, AC, attack, damage, delay, DPS, resists, and equipment.

Most raid targets respawn in **2 days and 18 hours**. Loot lockout matches that. The four elemental gods are **5 days and 18 hours**. Halls of Honor trials are **18 hours**. Plane of Time bosses are **6 days and 18 hours**. Gryme is **3 hours 8 minutes**. Aramin is **1 day**. Hedge and Xanamech are events. Evynd and Emmerik are **6 hours**. Storms lords are **6 hours**. The Keeper of Sorrows is **2 days 12 hours**. A wipe does not start those successful-kill timers. Scripted events use their own fail retries.

Do Phase 1 in any order. If you can only run one thing, do Justice first. That hail gates Halls of Honor and Bastion of Thunder.

### Phase 1 — Open the planes

1. **[Justice Trials](/strategy/justice_trials)** — Find **Mavuin**. Say **information**. Run Execution. Loot the Mark. Hail **The Tribunal**, say **mavuin sent me**. Hail Mavuin again. All six trials are only for [The Seventh Hammer](/strategy/the_seventh_hammer).
2. **[Plane of Disease](/strategy/plane_of_disease)** — Hail **Adler Fuirstel** outside the Disease stone. Say **ward**. Kill **[Gryme](/strategy/gryme_the_crypt_guardian)**, then **[Aramin](/strategy/aramin_the_spider_guardian)**, then **[Grummus](/strategy/grummus)**. Hail the Projection. Push the decaying pipe and drop into Crypt of Decay.
3. **[Plane of Innovation](/strategy/plane_of_innovation)** — **[Xanamech](/strategy/xanamech_nezmirthafen)** is one person for the factory door. Inside, hail **Giwin Mirakon**. Say **great warrior**, **task**, **test the machine**. Kill **[Manaetic Behemoth](/strategy/manaetic_behemoth)**. Hail Giwin at the east door. That is the Tactics flag.
4. **[Hedge Maze](/strategy/hedge_maze)** — Hail **Adroha Jezith** in Tranquility Sick Bay. Say **tortured by nightmares**. Keep Thelin alive. Kill **[A Construct of Nightmares](/strategy/a_construct_of_nightmares)**. Hail Thelin to zone out.
5. **[Plane of Valor](/strategy/plane_of_valor)** — One person needs a Crystalline Globe. Kill **[Aerin`Dar](/strategy/aerin_dar)**. Hail the Projection. You need the Justice trial for that hail to grant Halls of Honor. Drop the floor, clear the tunnel, click the lightning.
6. **Bastion of Thunder access** — Hail **Askr the Lost** in [Plane of Storms](/strategy/plane_of_storms) and finish his giant turn-ins. Click the shrine in Mount Grenidor. You need Askr's path and Mavuin. Steps are on [Bastion of Thunder](/strategy/bastion_of_thunder).

### Phase 2 — Crypt, Terris, Honor, Bastion

1. **[Crypt of Decay](/strategy/carprin_deatharn_event)** — Hail **Elder Fuirstel** in Sick Bay after Grummus. Kill the chapel: **[Carprin](/strategy/carprin_deatharn)** through **[High Priest Ultor Szanvon](/strategy/high_priest_ultor_szanvon)**. Hail **Tarkil Adan**. Click the bone throne. Kill **[Bertoxxulous](/strategy/bertoxxulous)**. Hail the Projection. Hail Elder Fuirstel again.
2. **[Terris-Thule](/strategy/terris_thule)** — Hedge flag opens the Lair. Kill Terris. Hail the Projection. Hail **Elder Poxbourne** in Sick Bay.
3. **[Halls of Honor Trials](/strategy/halls_of_honor_trials)** — All three. Hail **Trydan Faye** after **[Rydda`Dar](/strategy/rydda_dar)**. Hail **Rhaliq Trell** after the villager defense. Hail **Alekson Garn** after **[Advocent](/strategy/advocent_joran)**, **[Halgoz](/strategy/halgoz_rellinic)**, and **[Freegan](/strategy/freegan_haun)**. White mist on each hail.
4. **[Bastion of Thunder](/strategy/bastion_of_thunder)** — One person needs **Symbol of Torden**. **[Evynd Firestorm](/strategy/evynd_firestorm)**, then **[Emmerik Skyfury](/strategy/emmerik_skyfury)**, then **[Agnarr](/strategy/agnarr_the_storm_lord)**. Hail **Karana**. Say **follow the path of the Fallen**, then **send me**. Hail Maelin. Say **what lore**. You need Marr for that hail.

### Phase 3 — Saryrn, Marr, Zeks

1. **[Plane of Torment](/strategy/keeper_of_sorrows)** — You need Terris and Bertoxxulous plus the Fuirstel chain. Hail **Fahlia Shadyglade** in Sick Bay. Say **condition**, **black cube**, **plane of torment**, **will go**. Click the black cube. One person needs **Screaming Sphere**. Kill **[Maareq](/strategy/maareq_the_prophet)**, then **[The Keeper of Sorrows](/strategy/the_keeper_of_sorrows)**. Hail **Tylis Newleaf**. Kill **[Saryrn](/strategy/saryrn)**. Hail the Projection. Hail Fahlia. Hail Maelin.
2. **[Temple of Marr](/strategy/temple_of_marr)** — All three Honor trials. Kill **[Halon of Marr](/strategy/halon_of_marr)**, **[Edium, Guardian of Marr](/strategy/edium_guardian_of_marr)**, and **[Ralthazor, Champion of Marr](/strategy/ralthazor_champion_of_marr)**. Kill **[Lord Mithaniel Marr](/strategy/lord_mithaniel_marr)**. Hail the Projection. Hail Maelin.
3. **[Plane of Tactics](/strategy/plane_of_tactics)** — Behemoth flag. Click the Tactics stone. Kill **[Tallon Zek](/strategy/tallon_zek)** south, then **[Vallon Zek](/strategy/vallon_zek)** north. Hail each Projection. You need Maelin after Saryrn and Marr for those hails. Then hail Maelin. Say **what lore**. Kill **[Rallos Zek the Warlord](/strategy/rallos_zek_the_warlord)**. Hail the Projection in the arena. Hail Maelin. Say **what information**. You need the Agnarr flag for that hail. If all three die in one raid, hail Maelin before the Seer.

### Phase 4 — Elementals

Air, Earth, and Water open after Maelin has the Agnarr flag and the Rallos flag. Fire is the floor door in Solusek's chamber. You need Marr and Rallos, plus the five mini flags, to enter that chamber. If a Tranquility stone refuses you, bounce Seer and Maelin.

1. **[Tower of Solusek Ro](/strategy/tower_of_solusek_ro)** — Kill **[Xuzl](/strategy/xuzl)**, **[Arlyxir](/strategy/arlyxir)**, **[Jiva](/strategy/jiva)**, **[Rizlona](/strategy/rizlona)**, and **[The Protector of Dresolik](/strategy/the_protector_of_dresolik)**. Click the flaming cauldron after each. Click the extra shiny orange square. Kill **[Solusek Ro](/strategy/solusek_ro)**. Hail the Projection. Drop into the floor door for Plane of Fire.
2. **[Plane of Fire](/strategy/plane_of_fire)** — Kill **[Fennin Ro](/strategy/fennin_ro)**. Hail the Projection. Loot **Globe of Dancing Flame**.
3. **[Plane of Air](/strategy/plane_of_air)** — One person per group needs a **Wind Etched Key**. Kill **[Xegony](/strategy/xegony)**. Hail the Projection. Loot **Amorphous Cloud of Air**.
4. **[Plane of Water](/strategy/plane_of_water)** — Kill **[Coirnav](/strategy/coirnav)**. Hail **Essence of Water**. Loot **Sphere of Coalesced Water**.
5. **[Plane of Earth A](/strategy/plane_of_earth_a)** — One person needs **Gem-Etched Key** from Tantisala. Finish the four rings inside **24 hours**. Kill **[A Mystical Arbitor of Earth](/strategy/a_mystical_arbitor_of_earth)**. Hail the Projection. Loot **Passkey of the Twelve**. Click the door.
6. **[Plane of Earth B](/strategy/plane_of_earth_b)** — Kill the twelve Rathe Councilmen, then **[Avatar of Earth](/strategy/avatar_of_earth)**. Hail **Essence of Earth**. Loot **Mound of Living Stone**.

Fire, Air, Water, and Earth can be any order after you can enter them. Earth is the longest night.

### Phase 5 — Plane of Time

One person makes an **Odylic Vial** in a Tanaan kiln in Plane of Knowledge. It is not no-drop. Combine **windy glaze**, **aqua glaze**, **earthen glaze**, and **magma glaze** in a glaze mortar. Combine **1 block of clay**, **1 medium jar sketch**, and **1 flask of water** on a pottery wheel. Combine that container, **2 high quality firing sheets**, and the glaze in the kiln. Make each glaze from components in its elemental plane. Put **Globe of Dancing Flame**, **Amorphous Cloud of Air**, **Sphere of Coalesced Water**, and **Mound of Living Stone** in the vial and combine for **Quintessence of Elements**.

In [Plane of Innovation](/strategy/plane_of_innovation), say **yes** to **Chronographer Muan**. Say **researched** to **Loreseeker Maelin**. Click the machine.

Eighteen people per Phase 1 trial. Time A and B hold **72**. Time is a guild timeline. `#timelockout` shows your Plane of Time timeline. `#timelockout 1` through `#timelockout 6` show one phase. Click a trial portal to start the hour. Leftover time carries. Full event order is on [Plane of Time](/strategy/plane_of_time).

- Hail Maelin. Say **what lore**. You need Marr.

- Kill **[Saryrn](/strategy/saryrn)**. Hail the Projection.

### [Temple of Marr](/strategy/temple_of_marr)

### [Plane of Tactics](/strategy/plane_of_tactics)

### Elementals

- Air, Earth, and Water need the Agnarr and Rallos Maelin flags. Fire is the floor door after Solusek.
- Kill **[Fennin Ro](/strategy/fennin_ro)**. Hail the Projection. Loot **Globe of Dancing Flame**.
- *One person per group.* Wind Etched Key. Kill **[Xegony](/strategy/xegony)**. Hail the Projection. Loot **Amorphous Cloud of Air**.
- Kill **[Coirnav](/strategy/coirnav)**. Hail **Essence of Water**. Loot **Sphere of Coalesced Water**.
- *One person.* Gem-Etched Key. Finish the four Earth A rings. Kill **[A Mystical Arbitor of Earth](/strategy/a_mystical_arbitor_of_earth)**. Hail the Projection. Loot **Passkey of the Twelve**. Click the door.
- Kill the twelve Rathe Councilmen, then **[Avatar of Earth](/strategy/avatar_of_earth)**. Hail **Essence of Earth**. Loot **Mound of Living Stone**.
- If a Tranquility stone refuses you, bounce Seer and Maelin.

### [Plane of Time](/strategy/plane_of_time)

- *One person.* Odylic Vial in a Tanaan kiln. Four glazes in a glaze mortar. Clay, medium jar sketch, and water on a pottery wheel. That container, two high quality firing sheets, and the glaze in the kiln.
- Combine the four essences for **Quintessence of Elements**.
- Say **yes** to **Chronographer Muan**.
- Say **researched** to **Loreseeker Maelin**.
- Click the machine.
- Time A and B hold **72**. Phase 1 trials hold **18**.
- `#timelockout` shows your Plane of Time timeline. `#timelockout 1` through `#timelockout 6` show one phase.
