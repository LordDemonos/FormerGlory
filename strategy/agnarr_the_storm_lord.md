Phase 3 of [Bastion of Thunder](/strategy/bastion_of_thunder). He aggros on zone-in. Tanks go in first, healers right after.

Five storm portals. The middle one is not killable. Kite or offtank him until the other four portals and adds are dead. Portals recast every **120 seconds**, every **90 seconds** under 50%, and every **60 seconds** under 25%.

Stay on the platform. If he is pulled below it he ports back to spawn.

He is **Slowable** (25% mit). Dual Wield. Enrage. Hits 1406. 1.05M HP.

**Manastorm** is a PBAE (600 DD + 200 mana, Magic 0, AoE 150, 36s recast).

**Rage of the Rainkeeper** is a PBAE (800 DD + 80/tick + SpinStun + Levitate, Unresistable, AoE 50, 40s recast).

**Chain Lightning** is a proc (targeted AE, 500 DD + 500 mana, Magic 0).

Named adds spawn during the fight. Kill them as they appear:

- **Jolur Sandstorm** on pull — 240k HP, hits 803, Dual Wield, Slowable, poison immune
- **Ekil Thundercall** at 75% — 240k HP, hits 803, Dual Wield, Slowable, fire immune
- **Oljin Stormtide** at 50% — 240k HP, hits 803, Dual Wield, Slowable, cold immune
- **Hibdin Cyclone** at 25% — 240k HP, hits 803, Dual Wield, Reverse Slow, poison immune

On death, run up the steps to **Karana**. Hail him, say **follow the path of the Fallen** for the flag, then **send me** to zone out. He lasts 55 minutes and can give up to 72 flags, killer's raid or group only. You need Askr's Plane of Storms path, including the Bastion shrine, for that hail to grant the character flag.
