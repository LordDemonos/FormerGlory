End of the [Plane of Disease](/strategy/plane_of_disease) crypt.

He is **Unslowable**. He sees sneak. Sees Shroud of Stealth is random (10).

**Decay of the Plaguebringer** is a proc, not a cast. Group heals and disease cures help.

On death he spawns **A Planar Projection**. Hail it for the flag. The projection only answers the killer's raid or group, lasts 10 minutes, and can give up to 144 flags. Then push the decaying pipe in the room and drop down to [Crypt of Decay](/strategy/carprin_deatharn_event).
