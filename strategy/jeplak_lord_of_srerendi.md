South water camp in [Plane of Storms](/strategy/plane_of_storms). Clear every water giant. He is not targetable until that camp is dead.

He is **Unslowable**. Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 1246. Attack delay 18. 177k HP. Disease immune.

**Wind Strike** is a proc (75 DD + FD, Magic 0, 12s recast). Water giants can FD the tank. Adds spawn when someone dies.

Always drops **three Esoteric Medallions**.
