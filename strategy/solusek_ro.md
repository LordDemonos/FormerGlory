Click the extra shiny orange square in the center of [Tower of Solusek Ro](/strategy/tower_of_solusek_ro). You need all five mini flags and flags through [Rallos Zek the Warlord](/strategy/rallos_zek_the_warlord).

Two **Guardian of Fire** aggro at zone-up. Six total (130k, hits 1431). Dual Wield. **Slowable**. **Unity of Fire** (35 DS). Clear all six. They respawn in 58 minutes.

![Solusek-Ro-Zone-In](/assets/img/Solusek-Ro-Zone-In.jpg)

![Guardian-of-Fire](/assets/img/Guardian-of-Fire.jpg)

![Solusek-Ro-Platform](/assets/img/Solusek-Ro-Platform.jpg)

![Solusek-Ro-Door](/assets/img/Solusek-Ro-Door.jpg)

Keep the fight on the platform. If he is pulled down off the platform while under 50% he heals 100k and pulls idle **Guardian of Fire** onto him.

He is **Slowable**. Rampage. Dual Wield. Enrage. Hits 1913. Attack delay 9. 850k HP.

**Corona of Sol** is a self 150 DS. Dispel it. **Solar Flame** is a single (2000 DD + ATK -50, Fire -400, 24s recast). **Solar Winds** is a Targeted AE (1000 DD + ATK -50, Fire -400, AoE 150, 48s recast). Ranged DPS stay max range.

Hail the Planar Projection at the chamber entrance. Drop into the floor door next to it to zone into [Plane of Fire](/strategy/plane_of_fire).
