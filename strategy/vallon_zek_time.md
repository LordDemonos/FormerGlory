Phase 4. [Plane of Time](/strategy/plane_of_time). Kill **[Tallon Zek](/strategy/tallon_zek_time)** first so you have room.

He is **Unslowable**. Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 3000. Attack delay 11. Assist 100. 1.4M HP.

**Tactical Strike** is a single (FD + 30s stun, Unresistable, 24s recast). Multiple tanks. Spam heal. CH chain will not hold through the FD.

**Vallon's Precision** is a PBAE (100/tick + AC -59 + 40% agro, Unresistable, 35s recast). DPS watch aggro.

**45–50%:** two Vallon clones. Root or kite them. Kill clones, then finish Vallon.
