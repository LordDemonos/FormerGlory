There are six Plane of Justice trials. Completing **any one** trial is enough for planar progression flagging. Completing **all six** unlocks **[The Seventh Hammer](/strategy/the_seventh_hammer)**. Seventh Hammer is not required for planar progression. Full raid order is on [Planes of Power Progression](/strategy/pop_progression).

## Access

</a>

Find **Mavuin** in Plane of Justice. Hail him and say **information**.

Complete a trial and loot the Mark. Hail one of **The Tribunal** in the trial room and say **mavuin sent me**. Hail Mavuin again.

## Starting and leaving

Each Tribunal starts one trial. Hail them, say **prove**, then **prepared**. When you are ready, say **ready to begin the trial of [name]**. The start names are Execution, Flame, Lashing, **Stoning**, **Hanging**, and Torture.

![Justice-Ring](/assets/img/Justice-Ring.jpg)

If that trial is already running, the Tribunal tells you to wait.

To leave, hail Agent of The Tribunal and say **return**. If the trial is still underway, the Agent will not port you out. Fail lockout is 60 seconds. Success lockout is 30 minutes.

## Trial of Execution

Generally the easiest trial. Four waves of four mobs spawn. Kill one mob at a time and keep DPS up. A hooded executioner walks toward the victim / guillotines during each wave. If he reaches them, the trial fails.

The executioner only dies when all accompanying nemeses are dead.

After the fourth wave, **[Prime Executioner Vathoch](/strategy/prime_executioner_vathoch)** spawns. Kill him and loot Mark of Execution.

## Trial of Lashing

Four waves, then a boss. Each of the first four waves has four targetable mobs and one untargetable **a scourge of honor**. Kill the four targetable mobs. When they die, a flickering spirit spawns in each of the three hallways. Kill those, then the scourge despawns and the next wave can start.

After the fourth wave, **[Lashman Azakal](/strategy/lashman_azakal)** spawns. Kill him and loot Mark of Lashing.

## Trial of Stone

Start this one as **trial of stoning**. Mobs spawn from the doors. Four waves of four mobs. Spectres can be mezzed. Untargetable archers will not stop until nothing else stands — do not let the prisoners take more than they can bear. Those shooters despawn when the four targetable mobs die.

After the fourth wave, **[Yurae Zhaleem](/strategy/yurae_zhaleem)** spawns. Kill him and loot Mark of Stone.

## Trial of Suffocation

Start this one as **trial of hanging**. Waves spawn a few at a time. Kill **spirit of suffocation** mobs first or their victims perish and the trial fails.

After a few waves, **[Gallows Master Teion](/strategy/gallows_master_teion)** spawns instead of wraith of agony. Kill him and loot Mark of Suffocation. He is an Enchanter (Gravity Flux, mez, tash).

## Trial of Torture

Four waves of four mobs, two per side. After each wave a **wraith of agony** spawns in the middle room or the zone-in cubby. Kill it quickly or the trial fails. There is a short wait between waves.

On the fourth wave, **[Punisher Veshtaq](/strategy/punisher_veshtaq)** spawns instead of wraith of agony. Kill him and loot Mark of Torture. He is **Unslowable**.

## The Seventh Hammer

Do all six trials and loot the Mark from each. Say **knowledge** to one of The Tribunal in the trial area to receive The Mark of Justice. Hold The Mark of Justice on your cursor and click a blue portal behind a Tribunal to zone up (U can be needed). The Mark of Justice is not a key-ring item. One person per group can port the group.

![The-Mark-of-Justice](/assets/img/The-Mark-of-Justice.jpg)

**[The Seventh Hammer](/strategy/the_seventh_hammer)** is Unslowable, AE Rampages, hits 1240, and has 1.08M HP. Tremor of Judgment and Verdict of Eternity are cast by an invisible Tribunal on a ~3 minute cadence.
