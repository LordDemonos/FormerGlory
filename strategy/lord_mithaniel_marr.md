West of zone-in in [Temple of Marr](/strategy/temple_of_marr). If he is visible, he is up. He is untargetable until **Halon of Marr**, **Edium, Guardian of Marr**, and **Ralthazor, Champion of Marr** are dead. Pull them one at a time. Always Calls for Help — pull **An Honorable Protector** (100k) and **An Honorable Protector of Marr** (150k) out of the room first. They respawn in 12 to 15 minutes.

Keep the fight in his room. If he is pulled toward the hallway he ports home, wipes hate, and heals. Have someone other than the main tank eat Rampage.

He is **Slowable** (80% mit). Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 2096. Attack delay 12. 1.2M HP.

**Silence of Marr** is a single-target root (Magic -200, 3s recast). **Blinding Essence of Purity** is a 3s stun and blind (Magic -200, 60s recast). Interrupt **Touch of Nife** (1125 heal, 5s cast). **Strike of Marr** is a proc (3500 DD + 3s stun, Magic -150).

Hail the Planar Projection. It only answers the killer's raid or group, lasts 10 minutes, and can give up to 144 flags. You need all three [Halls of Honor Trials](/strategy/halls_of_honor_trials) flags for that hail. Then hail **Grand Librarian Maelin** in Plane of Knowledge.
