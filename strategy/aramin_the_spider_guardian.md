Second named on the [Plane of Disease](/strategy/plane_of_disease) path, further in the crypt after Gryme.

He is **permarooted** (run speed 0), so he can be pre-debuffed.

On death he spawns **7–8** spiders: **an arachnae serioun** (level 55) and **an arachnae maleveler** (level 60). Those adds depop after 10 minutes if nobody engages them.
