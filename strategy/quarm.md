Phase 6. [Plane of Time](/strategy/plane_of_time). 2 hours plus leftover.

He is **Slowable** (25% mit). AE Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 3800. Attack delay 38. 1.7M HP.

Mez **a time vortex**. They proc **Time Warp** (single, 50% slow + 50% spell haste, 36 disease). Cure disease.

![A-Time-Vortex](/assets/img/A-Time-Vortex.jpg)

**75%**, **50%**, **25%:** a head dies. **Balance of the Nameless** strips his debuffs. Slow him again. Debuff him again.

**Epoch Conviction** is a PBAE (20% slow + 20% spell haste + 100/tick + 100 mana/tick, Magic -300, 36 curse, 60s recast). Cure curse.

**Infernal Flames** is a PBAE (3000 DD, Fire -250, 60s recast).

**Glacier Breath** is a PBAE (100 DD + 20% snare, Cold -300, 36 curse, 60s recast).

**Venomed Mist** is a PBAE (500/tick + 500 mana/tick, Poison -300, 36 poison, 60s recast). Cure poison.

**Plagued Earth** is a PBAE (3000 DD + snare, Disease -250, 36 disease, 60s recast). Cure disease.

**Dark Aura** is a single (15% slow + 15% spell haste, Unresistable, 8 curse, 60s recast).

After loot, free **Zebuxoruk**. **Druzzil Ro** resets the timeline. The raid ports to Plane of Knowledge.
