Access is the [Hedge Maze](/strategy/hedge_maze). Clear trash to her chamber in The Lair of Terris Thule.

Keep the fight in her room. If she is pulled out of bounds she ports back to center.

**79% and 69%:** **a dream defiler** adds spawn — one per player who has damaged her. Some are mezzable, some are not.

**50%:** Direption of Dreams, an unresistable PBAE mass dispel.

**40%:** Four **a grotesque statue** gargoyles spawn (hit 1055, 53k HP, unmezzable). Off-tank or kite them and burn Terris.

On death she spawns **A Planar Projection**. Hail it for the flag. The projection only answers the killer's raid or group, lasts 10 minutes, and can give up to 72 flags. Then hail Elder Poxbourne in Plane of Tranquility.
