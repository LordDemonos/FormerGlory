Spider ring in [Plane of Air](/strategy/plane_of_air). Fight in the tunnel. Mez **An Erratic Arachnid**. Do not kill them. Kill **A Vorladien Archwalker** to spawn three **A Vorladien Spawn**. Three rounds. Then him under the rainbow. Erratic arachnids despawn. Then **[Avatar of Dust](/strategy/avatar_of_dust)**.

He is **Slowable** (25% mit). AE Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 1988. 600k HP.

**Wave of White Noise** is a PBAE (Silence, Unresistable, AoE 100, 45s recast). **Air Strike** is a proc (3500 DD, Unresistable, 30s recast).
