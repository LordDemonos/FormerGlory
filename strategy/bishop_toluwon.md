Spawns after **[Avhi Escron](/strategy/avhi_escron)** dies. [Crypt of Decay](/strategy/carprin_deatharn_event) chapel path.

He is **Permarooted** and **Slowable**. Flurry. Dual Wield. Hits 1777. 160k HP.

Casts **Touch of Life** (single, 10,000 heal, unresistable, 10s cast, 2s recast). Interrupt it. He can heal an ally, not only himself.

Trash in his room activates when he spawns.

On death he spawns **[High Priest Ultor Szanvon](/strategy/high_priest_ultor_szanvon)**, **[Vindor Mawnil](/strategy/vindor_mawnil)**, and **[Raex Pwodill](/strategy/raex_pwodill)**.
