Zone in from Plane of Tranquility.

One person needs **Gem-Etched Key** from **[Tantisala Jaggedtooth](/strategy/tantisala_jaggedtooth)** to open the tunnel door.

Complete the four ring events within 24 hours to spawn **[A Mystical Arbitor of Earth](/strategy/a_mystical_arbitor_of_earth)** in the caves. 50 minutes. Kill it. Hail the Planar Projection. Loot **Passkey of the Twelve**. Click the nearby door to zone into **[Plane of Earth B](/strategy/plane_of_earth_b)**.

Traps on the north field spawn trash.

## [Tantisala Jaggedtooth](/strategy/tantisala_jaggedtooth)

Loot **Gem-Etched Key**. He drops three.

![Tantisala-Jaggedtooth](/assets/img/Tantisala-Jaggedtooth.jpg)

He is **Slowable** (30% mit). AE Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 1910. 600k HP. **Diseased Cloud** is a Targeted AE (1250 DD + 55% slow, Disease -150, 12s recast).

## Stone ring — [Peregrin Rockskull](/strategy/peregrin_rockskull)

![Peregrin-Rockskull](/assets/img/Peregrin-Rockskull.jpg)

Stand along the east wall and pull.

Kill the eight mobs on the temple. Then **A Stone Fortification** packs (25k). AE them. Rampage. Then four **A Mound of Rubble** (105k). AE Rampage. Spread them. They walk back to the temple at low HP. Do not snare them. Then **A Rock Monstrosity** (800k, hits 1764). AE Rampage. Flurry. **Slowable** (30% mit). **Pebble Rain** is a PBAE (75 DD + 55% snare, Magic 0, AoE 50, 10s recast). Then **A Stone Heap** packs of four (33.5k). About six waves. Then **[Peregrin Rockskull](/strategy/peregrin_rockskull)**.

He is **Slowable** (30% mit). AE Rampage. Flurry. Dual Wield. Hits 1910. 600k HP. He memblurs. **Pebble Rain**.

## Mud ring — [A Monsterous Mudwalker](/strategy/a_monsterous_mudwalker)

![Mud-Temple](/assets/img/Mud-Temple.jpg)

![A-Monstrous-Mudwalker](/assets/img/A-Monstrous-Mudwalker.jpg)

Fight in the middle of the temple. Adds spawn on the raid.

Kill **An Earthen Mudwalker**. Then **A Sludge Lurker** (200k, hits 1011). AE Rampage. Flurry. **Slowable** (30% mit). At **75%**, **60%**, **35%**, **25%** he despawns and about ten mudlets spawn. Kill them. He comes back. Then four **A Filth Gorger** (80k, hits 926). Flurry. AE Rampage. Then **[A Monsterous Mudwalker](/strategy/a_monsterous_mudwalker)**.

He is **Slowable** (30% mit). AE Rampage. Flurry. Dual Wield. Hits 1910. 600k HP. He memblurs. **Shackles** is a PBAE (Root, Unresistable, AoE 35).

## Vine ring — [Derugoak Bloodwalker](/strategy/derugoak_bloodwalker)

![Derugoak-Bloodwalker](/assets/img/Derugoak-Bloodwalker.jpg)

Setup along the wall.

Kill about thirty **A Tainted Rock Beast**. Flurry. Rampage. Then ten **A Bloodthirsty Vegerog** (80k, hits 926). Flurry. Rampage. AE Rampage. Then **[Derugoak Bloodwalker](/strategy/derugoak_bloodwalker)**.

He is **Slowable** (30% mit). AE Rampage. Flurry. Dual Wield. Hits 1910. 600k HP. He memblurs. **Meteor Storm** is a Targeted AE (1500 DD + 500 mana + 1s stun, Fire -300, AoE 80, 40s recast).

## Dust ring — [A Perfected Warder of Earth](/strategy/a_perfected_warder_of_earth)

![Dusty-Ring](/assets/img/Dusty-Ring.jpg)

![A-Perfected-Warder-of-Earth](/assets/img/A-Perfected-Warder-of-Earth.jpg)

Pre-clear trash between camp and **a dusty warder** (46.4k). Kill the dusty warder to start. About five **A Dust Devotee** (39k, hits 803) spawn on him. Rampage. **Slowable** (30% mit). Pull the rest of the devotees out of the ring. About thirty.

Then three **Triumvirate of Soil** (85k, hits 970). AE Rampage. Flurry. Double Attack. **Slowable** (30% mit). They aggro the raid. Then **[A Perfected Warder of Earth](/strategy/a_perfected_warder_of_earth)**. At about **70%** three more Triumvirate spawn. Three **A Triumvirate Protector** (33.5k) also spawn. Mezzable. If the adds live, he HP locks at **50%**. Kill all six adds, then finish him.

He is **Slowable** (30% mit). AE Rampage. Rampage. Flurry. Double Attack. Dual Wield. Hits 1910. 600k HP. He memblurs. **Raging Blizzard** is a Targeted AE (600 DD + 400 mana, Cold -300, AoE 80, 40s recast).

## A Mystical Arbitor of Earth

He spawns in the caves after the fourth ring. 50 minutes. Do not drop combat.

He is **Slowable** (30% mit). AE Rampage. Dual Wield. Double Attack. Flurry. Hits 1988. Attack delay 13. 600k HP. He memblurs. If he leaves combat below 50% he warps to spawn and heals.

**Elemental Judgement** is a PBAE (500/tick + 350 mana/tick + spin stun + 35% slow, Magic -200, AoE 150, 36s recast). Melee stay max range.
