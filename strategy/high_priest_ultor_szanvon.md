Spawns when **[Bishop Toluwon](/strategy/bishop_toluwon)** dies. Untargetable until **[Raex Pwodill](/strategy/raex_pwodill)** and **[Vindor Mawnil](/strategy/vindor_mawnil)** are both dead. [Crypt of Decay](/strategy/carprin_deatharn_event) chapel path.

He is **Permarooted** and **Unslowable**. Dual Wield. Hits 1811. 250k HP.

Casts **Enveloping Entropy** (single, unresistable 90% slow + ATK -500, 36 disease counters, 60s recast). Cure it.

On death, hail **Tarkil Adan**. He lasts 10 minutes and can give up to 36 flags, killer's raid or group only. That flag opens the bone throne at zone-in.
