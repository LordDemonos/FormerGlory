Agnarr is a three-phase event. One person needs the Symbol of Torden on their cursor to click the statue that ports the raid up. Askr the Lost moves the raid between rooms after each phase.

**[Emmerik Skyfury](/strategy/emmerik_skyfury)** respawns in 6 hours. **[Agnarr the Storm Lord](/strategy/agnarr_the_storm_lord)** in **2 days and 18 hours**. **[Evynd Firestorm](/strategy/evynd_firestorm)** in 6 hours. Trash respawns in **19 minutes 30 seconds**.

Hail **Askr the Lost** in [Plane of Storms](/strategy/plane_of_storms). Turn in a Storm Giant Head. Say **it was me**, **paying attention**, then **continue** twice. Combine Storm Volaas Beard, Storm Satuur Sash, and Storm Taarid Bone in Askr's Bag of Verity and turn in the sealed bag.

Hail, **bastion of thunder**. Combine two Esoteric Medallions from different giant camps in the bag. Turn in Esoteric Meld.

Click the shrine in Mount Grenidor. You need that Askr path and Mavuin from the Justice trials. That grants Bastion of Thunder.

## Symbol of Torden

One person needs the Symbol. Combine these in an **Unadorned Symbol of Torden**:

- **Sandstorm Sphere** — jord
- **Lightning Sphere** — brann
- **Blizzard Sphere** — vann
- **Tornado Sphere** — vind

Each of the eight designated tower bosses drops **1 Unadorned Symbol of Torden** every time, including **Auliffe Chaoswind**, **Brynju Thunderclap**, **Eindride Icestorm**, and **Kuanbyr Hailstorm**. Those named respawn in 6 hours.

**Laef Windfall**, **Gaukr Sandstorm**, **Oreen Wavecrasher**, and **Hreidar Lynhillig** each drop **3 Rings of Torden** every time.

Selected elemental NPCs have a **25%** chance to carry their progression gem or sphere.

Hold **Symbol of Torden** on your cursor and click the statue. Your raid or group can click it for **5 minutes**. Clicking without that access: first **Magical energies shoot through your body**, then **You hear the cracking of stones around you**, then nearby storm watchers aggro.

![Askr-the-Lost](/assets/img/Askr-the-Lost.png)

Hold **Enchanted Ring of Torden** on your cursor and click a wing door to port your group (100 range) while you farm spheres.

## Phase 1

**[Evynd Firestorm](/strategy/evynd_firestorm)** is up on zone-in with three firestorm portals. Kite or offtank Evynd. Kill the portals and firestorm elementals first, then kill him. Portals recast every **120 seconds**.

![Evynd-Firestorm](/assets/img/Evynd-Firestorm.png)

![Bastion-Wing-1](/assets/img/Bastion-Wing-1.png)

He is **Slowable** (25% mit). Dual Wield. Enrage. Hits 928. Fire immune. 460k HP.

**Storm Flame** is a targeted AE (400 DD, Unresistable, AoE 40, 36s recast). **Flamewall** is a proc (targeted AE, 800/tick + AC -295, Fire 0).

On death, hail **Askr the Lost** and say **transport**. Askr lasts 55 minutes.

## Phase 2

**[Emmerik Skyfury](/strategy/emmerik_skyfury)** is up on zone-in with three celestial portals. Kite or offtank him while the raid kills portals and adds, then kill Emmerik. Portals recast every **120 seconds**, every **90 seconds** under 50%, and every **60 seconds** under 25%.

![Emmerik-Skyfury](/assets/img/Emmerik-Skyfury.png)

![Bastion-Wing-2-Portal](/assets/img/Bastion-Wing-2-Portal.jpg)

![Askr-the-Lost-Wing-2](/assets/img/Askr-the-Lost-Wing-2.jpg)

He is **Slowable** (25% mit). Dual Wield. Hits 1053. Cold and disease immune. 640k HP.

**Meteor Storm** is a targeted AE (1500 DD + 500 mana + 1s stun, Fire -300, 40s recast). **Storm Comet** is a targeted AE (3000 DD + 90% slow, Unresistable, 36 curse counters, 30s recast). **Stone Thunder** is a single-target root and silence (Magic -300).

Hail Askr, say **what storm**, and click **A Chaotic Vortex** to Agnarr. Askr lasts 55 minutes.

## Phase 3

**[Agnarr the Storm Lord](/strategy/agnarr_the_storm_lord)** aggros on zone-in. Tanks go in first, healers right after.

Five storm portals. The middle one is not killable. Kite or offtank Agnarr until the other four portals and adds are dead. Portals recast every **120 seconds**, every **90 seconds** under 50%, and every **60 seconds** under 25%.

Stay on the platform. If he is pulled below it he ports back to spawn.

He is **Slowable** (25% mit). Dual Wield. Enrage. Hits 1406. 1.05M HP.

**Manastorm** is a PBAE (600 DD + 200 mana, Magic 0, 36s recast). **Rage of the Rainkeeper** is a PBAE (800 DD + 80/tick + SpinStun + Levitate, Unresistable, 40s recast). **Chain Lightning** is a proc (targeted AE, 500 DD + 500 mana, Magic 0).

Named adds spawn during the fight. Kill them as they appear:

## After the kill

Run up the steps to **Karana**. Hail him, say **follow the path of the Fallen** for the flag, then **send me** to zone out. He lasts 55 minutes and can give up to 72 flags, killer's raid or group only. You need Askr's Plane of Storms path, including the Bastion shrine, for that hail to grant the character flag.
