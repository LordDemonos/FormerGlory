Plane of Torment is the tower crawl to flag Saryrn. The Keeper event is required for her Planar Projection.

Maareq and Saryrn respawn in 3 days. The Keeper of Sorrows 2 days 12 hours.

## Access

You need the **[Terris-Thule](/strategy/terris_thule)** flag and the **[Bertoxxulous](/strategy/bertoxxulous)** / Fuirstel chain finished. Hail **Fahlia Shadyglade** in Plane of Tranquility. Say **condition**, **black cube**, **plane of torment**, **will go**. Click the black cube over the reflecting pool.

Someone in the raid needs **Screaming Sphere** to open the tower and to port with Tylis. Kill the four Avatars for **Orb of Agony**, **Orb of Anguish**, **Orb of Pain**, and **Orb of Suffering**. Aggro **Baraguj Szuul** to port into his stomach. Drop down the floors to **An Unimaginable Horror**, kill it, then kill Baraguj (217k HP). Loot **Mouths of Baraguj Szuul** and combine the four orbs.

## Tower

One Screaming Sphere opens the door for the raid. Kill named on the way up: Salczek the Fleshgrinder, The Acolyte of Affliction, Ta`Grusch the Abomination.

![Saryrn-Tower-Door](/assets/img/Saryrn-Tower-Door.jpg)

![Saryrn-Tower](/assets/img/Saryrn-Tower.jpg)

**[Maareq the Prophet](/strategy/maareq_the_prophet)** is in the last room before Saryrn. He is **Permarooted** and **Slowable** (60% mit). Keep the fight in his room. Minions spawn every 5 seconds. As they die he gains Rampage, then Flurry, then delay 8.

## Keeper

When Maareq dies, Tylis Newleaf in the cage becomes targetable. Everyone stand under the cage. A flagged person hails him, says **will assist you**, then **we are ready**. Someone in the raid must have the Screaming Sphere.

![Tylis-Newleaf](/assets/img/Tylis-Newleaf.jpg)

Do not fall into the water or mud.

Right staircase, next door on the right and up, then the door straight ahead. Ramp across into the final chamber. Kill **[The Keeper of Sorrows](/strategy/the_keeper_of_sorrows)**. He is **Unslowable**. AE Rampage. Hits 973. 100k HP.

Hail Tylis. He lasts 10 minutes and can give up to 72 flags. Hail first. Then one person says **ready to return**.

## Saryrn

Pull **[Saryrn](/strategy/saryrn)** in her chamber. She is **Slowable** (70% mit). AE Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 573. Attack delay 10. 850k HP. Keep the fight in her room.

Casts Will of Saryrn (PBAE, 800 DD + 6s stun, Magic 0, 45s recast). Adds spawn at 99% and as she drops. Sorrowsong flies and sings from 99%. At 25% Sorrowsong becomes attackable. Kill it.

Hail the Planar Projection. It only answers the killer's raid or group, lasts 10 minutes, and can give up to 72 flags. You need the Tylis flag for that hail. Then hail Fahlia Shadyglade in Plane of Tranquility.
