North mini in [Temple of Marr](/strategy/temple_of_marr). Pull him after the Honorable Protectors. He can be pulled alone.

He is **Slowable** (60% mit). Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 1234. 200k HP.

**Blinding Beauty** is a single-target blind and 1s stun (Magic -150, 60s recast). **Denial** is a proc (1500 DD + 1s stun, Magic -150).

Kill all three minis. Then **[Lord Mithaniel Marr](/strategy/lord_mithaniel_marr)** is targetable.
