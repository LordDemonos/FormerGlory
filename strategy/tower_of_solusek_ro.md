You need flags through **[Rallos Zek the Warlord](/strategy/rallos_zek_the_warlord)** to enter Solusek Ro's chamber. Kill the five minis and click the flaming cauldron after each. Warders spawn on the path after a mini dies. Be in the room.

Five staircases from zone-in, left to right: **[Xuzl](/strategy/xuzl)**, **[Arlyxir](/strategy/arlyxir)**, **[Jiva](/strategy/jiva)**, **[Rizlona](/strategy/rizlona)**, **[The Protector of Dresolik](/strategy/the_protector_of_dresolik)**. Click the extra shiny orange square in the center for **[Solusek Ro](/strategy/solusek_ro)**.

The minis and Solusek respawn in 3 days.

## Xuzl

Far left staircase. Pull **a conjured automoton** (103.6k) out of the room.

Keep the fight in his room. If he is pulled into the hallway he ports home, wipes hate, and heals.

He is **Slowable**. Dual Wield. Enrage. Hits 1030. 600k HP. **Wrath of Xuzl** summons animated swords.

## Arlyxir

Second staircase from the left. Clear the room.

Keep the fight in her room. If she is pulled toward the hallway she ports home, wipes hate, and heals.

She is **Slowable**. Dual Wield. Enrage. Hits 1030. 750k HP. She is mezzable. **Unity of Fire** is 35 DS. Dispel it. At **12.5 minutes** she full heals and casts **Searing Flames**.

## Jiva

Center staircase. Clear **a guardian of Jiva** (80k, hits 882) in the hallway.

He is **Slowable**. Dual Wield. Enrage. **Permarooted**. Always Calls for Help. Hits 1030. 700k HP. Adds every **45 seconds**. Kill them. **Reckless Mutterings** is a PBAE (500 DD + ATK -50). **Draught of Jiva** proc (688 DD + stun).

## Rizlona

Second staircase from the right. Clear **a guardian of Rizlona** (26.5k).

Keep the fight in her room. If she is pulled out she ports home, wipes hate, and heals.

Human form is **Slowable**. Dual Wield. Hits 843. 300k HP. **Flame Song of Ro** is a PBAE (850/tick). On death she spawns as a dragon. Dragon hits 1515. 600k HP. Dual Wield. Does not See IVU. Not Immune to melee except magical. **Lava Breath** is a Targeted AE (500 DD + dispel). Recast buffs.

## The Protector of Dresolik

Far right staircase.

Four **Guardian of Dresolik** (285k, hits 770) in the chamber. Rampage. Dual Wield. **Slowable**. **Fist of Lava** proc. They port home if pulled out of the room. The last death spawns the Protector.

He is **Slowable**. Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 1030. 800k HP. **Fist of Lava** proc (150 DD + 3s stun).

## Solusek Ro

Click the extra shiny orange square in the center. You need all five mini flags.

Two **Guardian of Fire** aggro at zone-up. Six total (130k, hits 1431). Dual Wield. **Slowable**. **Unity of Fire** (35 DS). Clear all six. They respawn in 58 minutes.

Keep **[Solusek Ro](/strategy/solusek_ro)** on the platform. If he is pulled down under 50% he heals 100k and pulls idle Guardians onto him.

He is **Slowable**. Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 1913. Attack delay 9. 850k HP. Dispel **Corona of Sol** (150 DS). **Solar Flame** is a 2000 DD. **Solar Winds** is a Targeted AE (1000 DD). Ranged DPS stay max range.
