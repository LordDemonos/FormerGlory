Kill **[Muzlakh the Chosen](/strategy/muzlakh_the_chosen)** at zone-up. Clear **High Councilman of the Queen** and **A Prismatic Guardian of Xegony** in her room. Kill **[Xegony the Queen of Air](/strategy/xegony)**. Hail the Planar Projection. Loot **Amorphous Cloud of Air**. Combine it with the other three elemental essences in an **Odylic Vial** for Plane of Time.

Xegony respawns in 3 days. Muzlakh respawns in 3 days 12 hours.

## Wind Etched Key

Say **Plane of Air** to **Sarhya the Dawnbreeze** at the Plane of Air stone in Plane of Tranquility for **Pouch of Swirling Winds**. Combine the four Mystical Essences in it.

- Stormrider ring → **[Avatar of Wind](/strategy/avatar_of_wind)** → Mystical Essence of Wind
- Elemental ring → **[Avatar of Smoke](/strategy/avatar_of_smoke)** → Mystical Essence of Smoke
- Phoenix ring → **[Avatar of Mist](/strategy/avatar_of_mist)** → Mystical Essence of Mist
- Spider ring → **[Avatar of Dust](/strategy/avatar_of_dust)** → Mystical Essence of Dust

All four Avatars are **Slowable** (25% mit). AE Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 2060. 750k HP.

## The room

She **Always Calls for Help**. Assist 135. Clear the room.

Six **A Prismatic Guardian of Xegony** (365k, hits 1145). Rampage. Dual Wield. **Slowable** (25% mit). Assist 135. **Bellowing Winds** is a PBAE (250/tick + spin, Magic -100, AoE 300, 12s recast). They respawn in 4 days.

Three **High Councilman of the Queen** (183k, hits 887). Rampage. AE Rampage. Dual Wield. **Slowable** (25% mit). They respawn in 4 days.

## Xegony the Queen of Air

Tank **[Xegony the Queen of Air](/strategy/xegony)** away from the raid. Park the raid behind her, away from add spawns. A kite team grabs adds so they do not run the tank.

She is **Slowable** (25% mit). AE Rampage. Dual Wield. Double Attack. Hits 2440. Attack delay 10. 1.5M HP.

**Wind of Xegony** is a PBAE (1000 DD + 200/tick + 30% slow, Magic -400, 13 curse, AoE 100, 30s recast). Cure curse.

Six add waves: on engage, then **85%**, **70%**, **55%**, **40%**, **25%**. Kill the wave, then go back to her. Waves can spawn in different orders. Adds are mezzable.

- Iron Knights — **Rindaler Egulrtan** (75k, hits 675). Rampage. **Wave of White Noise** (PBAE, Silence, Unresistable, AoE 100, 45s recast).
- Spiders — **Huridaf Vorgaasna** (178k, hits 1312). Rampage. **Wave of White Noise**.
- Elementals — **An Elemental Arbitor** (75.4k, hits 675). Rampage. **Diseased Cloud** (Targeted AE, 1250 DD + 55% slow, Disease -150, 12s recast).
- Bugs — **Wesreh Galleantan** (75k, hits 675). Rampage. **Diseased Cloud**.
- Efreeti — **Nuquernal Belegrodian** (75k, hits 675). Rampage. **Wave of White Noise**.
- Phoenixes — **Weruis Herfadban** (75k, hits 675). Rampage. Double Attack. **Diseased Cloud**.

Hail the Planar Projection. Loot **Amorphous Cloud of Air**.

## Ring events

Clear the island to start each ring. Avatars shout when they spawn.

**Stormrider — [Avatar of Wind](/strategy/avatar_of_wind).** Stormriders go live in pairs. Kill **Stormrider Priest of Destruction** (157k, hits 1390). Wait 5 to 10 minutes. Kill the low-level **Pherlondien Clawpike** trash (27.4k) and the named (550k, hits 1764). Dual Wield. **Slowable** (25% mit). Then **Avatar of Wind**. **Lure of the Storm** is a PBAE (800 DD + dispel + spin, Magic -250, 36s recast). **Cyclone** is a single (200/tick + 1s stun + 95% agro, Magic -150). **Tornado** is a proc (Single, 200/tick + 1s stun, Magic -100).
