Earth trial. [Plane of Time](/strategy/plane_of_time). 18 people. 1 hour for all five trials.

Three **a pile of living rubble**. Unmezzable. Unrootable. Tank them. Each death spawns **a rock shaped assassin**. Mezzable. Unrootable. Kill the assassins before the next rubble.

**Terlok of Earth** spawns. Kill him. Finish leftover trash.

He is **Slowable** (25% mit). Dual Wield. Hits 1155. Attack delay 16. 250k HP.
