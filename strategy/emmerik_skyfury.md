Phase 2 of [Bastion of Thunder](/strategy/bastion_of_thunder). He is up on zone-in with three celestial portals.

Kite or offtank him. Kill the portals and adds first, then kill him. Portals recast every **120 seconds**, every **90 seconds** under 50%, and every **60 seconds** under 25%.

He is **Slowable** (25% mit). Dual Wield. Double Attack. Hits 1053. Cold and disease immune. 640k HP.

**Meteor Storm** is a targeted AE (1500 DD + 500 mana + 1s stun, Fire -300, AoE 80, 40s recast).

**Storm Comet** is a targeted AE (3000 DD + 90% slow, Unresistable, 36 curse counters, AoE 30, 30s recast).

**Stone Thunder** is a single-target root and silence (Magic -300).

On death, hail **Askr the Lost**, say **what storm**, and click **A Chaotic Vortex** to Agnarr. Askr lasts 55 minutes.
