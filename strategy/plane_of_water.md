Zone in from Plane of Tranquility.

<a href="/assets/img/Coirnav-Map.jpg" target="_blank">
  <img src="/assets/img/Coirnav-Map.jpg" alt="Coirnav-Map"/>
</a>

Kill **[Guardian of Coirnav](/strategy/guardian_of_coirnav)** to start. 14 minutes. Root the wave nameds off the raid. AE the adds. Kill the nameds when they respawn at Coirnav. Kill **[Coirnav the Avatar of Water](/strategy/coirnav)**. Hail **Essence of Water**. Loot **Sphere of Coalesced Water**. Combine it with the other three elemental essences in an **Odylic Vial** for Plane of Time.

If the timer expires, the raid ports to Plane of Knowledge.

Coirnav respawns in 3 days. Guardian of Coirnav respawns in 4 days.

## Waves

Three waves. Each named comes with 20 to 25 adds. Root the named off the raid. Debuff him. Do not put him into summon. Pull the adds. Aggro 1. AE them. Adds are mezzable. **Slowable** (25% mit).

**Wave 1 — [Pwelon of Vapor](/strategy/pwelon_of_vapor)** and **a triloun vaporfiend** (7.4k, hits 457). He is **Slowable** (50% mit). Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 1373. 2M HP. Root him off the raid.

## Coirnav the Avatar of Water

Once the nameds are dead, **[Coirnav the Avatar of Water](/strategy/coirnav)** becomes targetable. More adds spawn. Offtank the adds. Burn him.

He is **Slowable** (95% mit). AE Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 1653. Attack delay 15. 3M HP. Run speed 0. He **Always Calls for Help**. Assist 100. Melee stay max range.

**Curse of the Triumvirate** is a PBAE (2000 DD + dispel + ATK -500, Cold -700, 18 curse, AoE 100, 30s recast). Cure curse.

## Fish summoning

![Fishlord-Craiyk](/assets/img/Fishlord-Craiyk.jpg) ![Fishlord-Lezom](/assets/img/Fishlord-Lezom.jpg)

<a href="/assets/img/Fishlord-Map.jpg" target="_blank">
  <img src="/assets/img/Fishlord-Map.jpg" alt="Fishlord-Map"/>
</a>

Give 100pp to **Fishlord Craiyk** or **Fishlord Lezom**. Each tunnel is its own event. Keep the Fishlord alive. Waves of fish spawn. Named anglerfish scale up. Trash is mezzable and **Slowable**. If the Fishlord dies, you fail.
