Optional factory-door event in the [Plane of Innovation](/strategy/plane_of_innovation) junkyard. Not required for planar progression. One person with the flag opens the factory for the raid.

![Nitram-Anizok](/assets/img/Nitram-Anizok.jpg)

Give **Copper Node**, **Bundle of Super Conductive Wires**, and **Intact Power Cell** to **Nitram Anizok**. He paths to the northeast. Pre-clear the route. Keep Nitram alive.

He starts lying down and untargetable. He despawns in 30 minutes if nobody engages. Nitram powers it, then it wakes and aggroes. He is **Slowable**. Dual Wield. Hits 1390.

**Steam Blast** is a large PBAE (800 DD, Fire -300). **Electrical Short** is a targeted AE rain (300 DD + 1s stun, Magic -200). **Oil Spray** is an unresistable 65% snare PBAE.

On death, hail Nitram. He lasts 10 minutes and can give up to 72 flags, killer's raid or group only. He tells you to twist the bottom rivet of the factory door icon three times to the right.
