**[Lord Mithaniel Marr](/strategy/lord_mithaniel_marr)** is west of zone-in. If he is visible, he is up. He is untargetable until all three mini bosses are dead. The minis and Marr respawn in 3 days.

## The room

Pull **An Honorable Protector** (100k) and **An Honorable Protector of Marr** (150k) out of the room first. Always Calls for Help. They respawn in 12 to 15 minutes.

The minis can be pulled one at a time. Kill all three.

**[Halon of Marr](/strategy/halon_of_marr)** — North. Beastlord. **Slowable** (60% mit). Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 1234. 200k HP. **Blinding Beauty** is a single-target blind and 1s stun (Magic -150, 60s recast). **Denial** is a proc (1500 DD + 1s stun, Magic -150).

## Lord Mithaniel Marr

Once all three are dead he is targetable. Keep the fight in his room. If he is pulled toward the hallway he ports home, wipes hate, and heals. Have someone other than the main tank eat Rampage.

He is **Slowable** (80% mit). Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 2096. Attack delay 12. 1.2M HP.

**Silence of Marr** is a single-target root (Magic -200, 3s recast). **Blinding Essence of Purity** is a 3s stun and blind (Magic -200, 60s recast). Interrupt **Touch of Nife** (1125 heal, 5s cast). **Strike of Marr** is a proc (3500 DD + 3s stun, Magic -150).

## After the kill

Hail the Planar Projection. It only answers the killer's raid or group, lasts 10 minutes, and can give up to 144 flags. You need all three Halls of Honor trial flags for that hail. Then hail **Grand Librarian Maelin** in Plane of Knowledge.
