Outdoor named in [Plane of Air](/strategy/plane_of_air).

He is **Slowable** (25% mit). AE Rampage. Hits 1312. 175k HP.

**Elnerick's Entombment of Ice** is a cast (Single, 303 (L39) to 355 (L65) + Root, Cold -10, 3.5s recast). **Atol's Spectral Shackles** is a cast (Targeted AE, Decrease Movement by 60%, Magic 0, 2.25s recast). **Inferno of Al'Kabor** is a cast (Targeted AE, 750 (L53) to 800 (L58), Fire -10, 9s recast). **Pillar of Flame** is a cast (Targeted AE, 937 (L57) to 952 (L60), Fire -10, 6s recast). **Invert Gravity** is a cast (Targeted AE, 368 (L59) to 380 (L65) + Toss Up by 350, Magic 0, 12s recast). **Greater Fetter** is a cast (Single, Root, Magic -30, 2.25s recast). He also casts wizard nukes, roots, and lures. Interrupt the AEs.
