Kill the twelve **A Rathe Councilman**. **[Avatar of Earth](/strategy/avatar_of_earth)** spawns. 35 minutes. Kill it. Hail **Essence of Earth**. Loot **Mound of Living Stone**. Combine it with the other three elemental essences in an **Odylic Vial** for Plane of Time.

## The Rathe Council

Twelve councilmen. 300k each. Hits 2964 at full HP. Hits drop as they lose HP: **75%**, **50%**, **25%**, **11%** (850). Dual Wield. **Slowable** (30% mit). Each **Always Calls for Help**. Assist 250. **Earthen Vengeance** is a PBAE (1000 DD + 200/tick + 20% slow, Magic -400, 4 curse, AoE 100, 60s recast). Cure curse. Spread them. Six tanks.

Six are mezzable. Six are **Unmezzable**. Root the Unmezzable ones until a tank takes them.

Mez team in the drop-down tunnel. Tank team across the water around the temple. Pull one mezzable at a time. DPS to **5–10%**. Mez. Do not let them drop combat. A 10 minute mez wipes hate. They teleport a random client every 60 seconds unless mezzed or under 11%.

Then tank the six Unmezzable one at a time to **5–10%**. Keep them spread. When all twelve are low, kill them. When the last dies, **Avatar of Earth** spawns in the middle.

## Avatar of Earth

He is **Slowable** (70% mit). Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 1958. Attack delay 11. 500k HP.

**Ashen Form** is a single (95% agro, Magic -350, 60s recast). Watch aggro.

**Marl** is a PBAE (100/tick, Magic -350, 36 curse, AoE 300, 60s recast). Cure curse.

Hail **Essence of Earth**. It only answers the killer's raid or group, lasts 20 minutes, and can give up to 72 flags. Loot **Mound of Living Stone**.
