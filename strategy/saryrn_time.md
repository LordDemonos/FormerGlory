Phase 4. [Plane of Time](/strategy/plane_of_time).

She is **Unslowable**. Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 1853. Attack delay 12. 1.4M HP.

Four adds at about **90%**, **50%**, and **10%**. Looking into the room, right side mezzable. Left side tank or CC. Kill them as they spawn or hold them to the end.

**Torrent of Agony** is a single (1500 DD + 60% agro + 40% slow, Poison -300, 20 disease, 24s recast). Cure disease.

**Horrifying affliction** is a PBAE (50% spell damage + 500 mana + 175/tick mana, Disease -300, 30s recast).

**Torrential Torment** is a PBAE (45 mana/tick, Poison -450, 9 curse, 5 min recast). Cure curse.
