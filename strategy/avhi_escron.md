Spawns after **[Carprin Deatharn](/strategy/carprin_deatharn)** dies. [Crypt of Decay](/strategy/carprin_deatharn_event) chapel path.

He is **Permarooted** and **Unslowable**. Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 1583. 120k HP.

Procs **Gathering of Souls** (PBAE, 600/tick, 30s, Magic 0, AoE 250).

Four **reanimated reavers** spawn with him (18.1k HP, hit 471, corpse campers). Four more spawn whenever someone leaves his hate list — death, FD, zone, or mem-blur. Keep hate on him. Kill the reavers.

On death he spawns **[Bishop Toluwon](/strategy/bishop_toluwon)**.
