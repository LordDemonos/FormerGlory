Center staircase in [Tower of Solusek Ro](/strategy/tower_of_solusek_ro). Clear **a guardian of Jiva** (80k, hits 882) in the hallway.

He is **Slowable**. Dual Wield. Enrage. **Permarooted**. Always Calls for Help. Hits 1030. Attack delay 16. 700k HP.

Every **45 seconds** he calls an add (Makin, Adeel, Jiri, Seif, Nabil, Aydin, Cemal, Fahim) and says their name. Kill the adds as they spawn.

**Reckless Mutterings** is a PBAE (500 DD + ATK -50, Fire -300, AoE 200, 20s recast). **Draught of Jiva** is a proc (688 DD + stun, Magic 0).

On death a **flaming cauldron** spawns. Click it. Three **a warder of Jiva** spawn on the path. Be in the room before the kill.
