**<a href="/strategy/aerin_dar">Aerin`Dar</a>** is the Plane of Valor raid. One person needs a Crystalline Globe to open the lair door. After the kill, hail the Planar Projection, then take the underground tunnel to Halls of Honor.

He respawns in 3 days.

## Crystalline Globe

Only one person needs the globe. Hail **Sergeant Johson Popoah** at the camp east of zone-in, then hail him again.

**Paralin Notion:** hail, **brave**, **company**, **squad**, **assistance**, **things**.

**Master Sergeant Aaramis Daryln** in the fort: hail, **aid the cause**.

Paralin again: hail, **prove**. Turn in **A Crystalline Spider's Heart** (crystalline arachnae), **A Razorfiend's Heart** (west caves), and **A Planarian Larvae's Heart**.

Aaramis: hail, **glass lair**, **aerin**, **company**, **mission**.

**Captain Ryglot Cupperhide:** hail, **busy**, **help**. Turn in the three globe pieces:

- **A Crystalline Orb** — A Luminii Crawler (rare among crystalline crawlers)
- **A Crystalline Staff** — A Gamite Frog
- **A Crystalline Gem** — An Undead Vassal or Sergeant Terrick Burns

He gives **A Crystalline Globe**. Click the lair door with it. Keep the globe. Invis to that door.

## The fight

Past the door is a prep room. Click the orb to port into the lair. The orb only works if **<a href="/strategy/aerin_dar">Aerin`Dar</a>** is up.

![Aerin-Dar-Prep-Room](/assets/img/Aerin-Dar-Prep-Room.jpg)

Pull him off the center platform. That floor opens after he dies. Hail the Planar Projection first or people fall before they flag.

He is **Slowable**. Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 1347. **Glass Shards** is a 3000 DD proc with a 45% slow. **Glass Roar** is a PBAE (700 DD + 500/tick + Fear up to level 52).

Eight unmezzable **a mindless minion** and mezzable **Rahlgon** become targetable during the fight. Root the minions. Mez Rahlgon.

## After the kill

Hail the Planar Projection. It lasts 10 minutes and can give up to 72 flags, killer's raid or group only. You need a completed Justice trial for that hail to grant Halls of Honor.

Drop down the center floor. Clear the tunnel. Click the lightning at the end to zone into [Halls of Honor](/strategy/halls_of_honor_trials). If you skip the projection hail, the portal says you lack the will to pass through safely.
