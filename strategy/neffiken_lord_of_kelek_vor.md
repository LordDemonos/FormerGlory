Northeast forest camp in [Plane of Storms](/strategy/plane_of_storms). Clear every forest giant. He is not targetable until that camp is dead.

He is **Unslowable**. Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 1481. Attack delay 16. 305k HP. Cold immune.

Treants spawn when he takes damage. Split them or kill them. **Manabolt** is a proc (1500 mana + 100 stam, Unresistable, 18s recast).

Always drops **three Esoteric Medallions**.
