Complete the four ring events in [Plane of Earth A](/strategy/plane_of_earth_a) within 24 hours. He spawns in the caves. 50 minutes. Kill **[Tantisala Jaggedtooth](/strategy/tantisala_jaggedtooth)** for **Gem-Etched Key** to open the tunnel door.

He is **Slowable** (30% mit). AE Rampage. Dual Wield. Double Attack. Flurry. Hits 1988. Attack delay 13. 600k HP. He memblurs. Do not drop combat. If he leaves combat below 50% he warps to spawn and heals.

**Elemental Judgement** is a PBAE (500/tick + 350 mana/tick + spin stun + 35% slow, Magic -200, AoE 150, 36s recast). Melee stay max range.

Hail the Planar Projection. It only answers the killer's raid or group, lasts 10 minutes, and can give up to 54 flags. Loot **Passkey of the Twelve**. Click the nearby door to zone into [Plane of Earth B](/strategy/plane_of_earth_b).
