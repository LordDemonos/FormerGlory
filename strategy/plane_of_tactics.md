Drunder, the Fortress of Zek has three raids: **[Tallon Zek](/strategy/tallon_zek)** south, **[Vallon Zek](/strategy/vallon_zek)** north, and **[Rallos Zek the Warlord](/strategy/rallos_zek_the_warlord)** in the halls above the arena then the pit.

Click the Tactics portal in Plane of Tranquility. Hail **Grand Librarian Maelin** in Plane of Knowledge after [Lord Mithaniel Marr](/strategy/lord_mithaniel_marr).

Tallon and Vallon respawn in 3 days. Rallos 3 days.

## Tallon Zek

South wing. Pull trash out of the corners. Levitate. The door ports to zone-in when he is down.

<a href="/assets/img/Tallon-Vallon-Spawn-Map.png" target="_blank">
  <img src="/assets/img/Tallon-Vallon-Spawn-Map.png" alt="Tallon-Vallon-Spawn-Map"/>
</a>

He is **Unslowable**. Dual Wield. Enrage. **Permarooted**. Hits 955. 440k HP. Every **20 seconds** he shadowsteps and wipes hate.

**Barb of Tallon** chain-casts (Cold slow+snare cone, Fire DD cone, Disease 300/tick cone, Poison single DD). Spread. **Tallon's Balance** is -300 resists. **Avatar Power** dispels levitate.

## Vallon Zek

North wing. Pull trash out of the corners. Keep the fight in his room. The door ports to zone-in when he is down.

He is **Slowable**. Dual Wield. Enrage. Hits 955. 175k HP on the first Vallon.

**50%:** five Vallons spawn. One real (84k, casts). Four fakes (42k, no casts). Kill fakes, then the real one. The 84k reals do not Dual Wield. The real one splits the same way at 50% until the last wave.

**Vallon's Precision** is a PBAE (100/tick + AC -59 + 40% agro, Unresistable). **Vallon's Subversion** is a 60% agro proc.

## Rallos Zek the Warlord

Do not drop into the pit from zone-in. Go around the arena and up the stairs. Clear both halls.

Kill **Decorin Berik** (69.4k, hits 831) and **Decorin Grunhork** (69.6k, hits 831). Dual Wield. Enrage. They respawn in 5 minutes. Both must be dead at the same time. Do not kill them on the twin spawn points.

Event Tallon and Vallon spawn in the halls, then roam toward Fake Rallos. Pull them apart. Kill both.

Event Tallon is the same as the south-wing Tallon (440k, Unslowable, Barbs, shadowstep). Levitate.

Event Vallon is **Slowable**. Flurry. Dual Wield. Hits 1053. 225k HP. He splits. Kill the real Vallon. Offtank the add.

**Rallos Zek** (Fake) — **Slowable** (75% mit). AE Rampage. Dual Wield. Enrage. Hits 1072. 750k HP. **Sedition** proc (AC -442). Tank in a corner. Melee at max range. He despawns in the 50s and the Warlord spawns in the arena.
