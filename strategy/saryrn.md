Top of Saryrn's tower in [Plane of Torment](/strategy/keeper_of_sorrows). Do **[The Keeper of Sorrows](/strategy/the_keeper_of_sorrows)** first. You need that Tylis flag for her Planar Projection.

She is **Slowable** (70% mit). AE Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 573. Attack delay 10. 850k HP. Keep the fight in her chamber. If she is pulled down she ports back and heals.

Casts **Will of Saryrn** (PBAE, 800 DD + 6s stun, Magic 0, AoE 50, 45s recast).

**99%:** Sorrowsong starts flying the room and casts Sorrow Song. Adds spawn.

Adds spawn again as she drops through 90%, 80%, 70%, 60%, 50%, 40%, 30%, 20%, and 10%. Kill or mez them.

**25%:** Sorrowsong becomes attackable (116k HP, hits 805, Flurry, Dual Wield) and flies onto her. Kill it.

Hail the Planar Projection. It only answers the killer's raid or group, lasts 10 minutes, and can give up to 72 flags. You need the Tylis flag for that hail. Then hail Fahlia Shadyglade in Plane of Tranquility.
