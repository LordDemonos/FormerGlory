Access is on the [Justice Trials](/strategy/justice_trials) page. Complete all six trials, say **knowledge** to a Tribunal for The Mark of Justice, then click a blue portal with the mark on your cursor.

Say **ready to face** to start, or he aggros with "Prepare for judgement mortals!"

He is **Unslowable**. He sees sneak. He does not see Shroud of Stealth.

Tremor of Judgment and Verdict of Eternity are cast by an invisible Tribunal, not from his own spell list. First Tremor is ~86–95 seconds in, then about every 187 seconds. First Verdict is ~181–190 seconds in, then about every 187 seconds.
