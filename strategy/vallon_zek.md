North wing of [Plane of Tactics](/strategy/plane_of_tactics). Pull trash out of the corners before the raid enters. Keep the fight in his room. If he is pulled toward the hallway he ports home. The door ports you back to zone-in when he is down. If he is up it says the doors are held shut by magical forces.

He is **Slowable**. Dual Wield. Enrage. Hits 955. Attack delay 14. 175k HP on the first Vallon.

**50%:** he despawns and five Vallons spawn. One is real (84k HP, hits 842, casts, Summon). Four are fakes (42k HP, hits 622, no casts). Kill fakes first, then the real one. The 84k reals do not Dual Wield. The real one splits the same way at 50% until the last wave. Then kill that real one to 0.

The real one casts **Vallon's Precision** (PBAE, 100/tick + AC -59 + 40% agro, Unresistable, AoE 75, 35s recast). **Vallon's Subversion** is a proc (single-target 60% agro, Unresistable).

Hail the Planar Projection. It only answers the killer's raid or group, lasts 10 minutes, and can give up to 72 flags. You need Grand Librarian Maelin after [Lord Mithaniel Marr](/strategy/lord_mithaniel_marr) for that hail. Then hail Maelin in Plane of Knowledge.
