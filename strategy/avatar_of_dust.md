Spider ring in [Plane of Air](/strategy/plane_of_air). Fight in the tunnel. Mez **An Erratic Arachnid**. Do not kill them. Kill **A Vorladien Archwalker** waves, then **[Sigismond Windwalker](/strategy/sigismond_windwalker)**. He spawns in the temple. Loot **Mystical Essence of Dust**.

He is **Slowable** (25% mit). AE Rampage. Dual Wield. Corpse Camper. Hits 2060. Attack delay 9. 750k HP.

**Wave of White Noise** is a PBAE (Silence, Unresistable, AoE 100, 45s recast). **Chain Lightning** is a proc (Targeted AE, 500 DD + 500 mana, Magic 0, AoE 30).
