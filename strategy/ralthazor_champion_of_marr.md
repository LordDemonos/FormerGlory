Front of Marr in [Temple of Marr](/strategy/temple_of_marr). Pull him after the Honorable Protectors. He can be pulled alone.

He is **Slowable** (65% mit). Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 1600. 275k HP.

**Champion's Protection** is a PBAE (65% snare + 40% slow, Magic -150, AoE 100, 180s recast). **Strike of the Champion** is a proc (1000 DD + Root + 1s stun, Magic -150).

Kill all three minis. Then **[Lord Mithaniel Marr](/strategy/lord_mithaniel_marr)** is targetable.
