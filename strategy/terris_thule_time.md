Phase 4. [Plane of Time](/strategy/plane_of_time).

She is **Unslowable**. Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 1801. Attack delay 11. 1.1M HP.

Four adds at about **90%**, **50%**, and **10%**. Looking into the room, right side mezzable. Left side tank or CC. Some adds mez.

**Quivering Nightmares** is a PBAE (25% spell/bash hate + STR/AGI/STA/INT/WIS -200, Magic -400, 24s recast).

**Phantasmal Torment** is a PBAE (200/tick + 150 mana/tick, Magic -300, 30s recast).

**Insipid Dreams** is a PBAE (1200 DD + 4s stun, Fire -450, 60s recast).
