South wing of [Plane of Tactics](/strategy/plane_of_tactics). Pull trash out of the corners before the raid enters. Levitate. The door ports you back to zone-in when he is down. If he is up it says the doors are held shut by magical forces.

He is **Unslowable**. Dual Wield. Enrage. **Permarooted**. Hits 955. Attack delay 18. 440k HP.

Every **20 seconds** he shadowsteps and wipes hate. Tanks reaggro.

**Barb of Tallon** chain-casts. Targeted AE Cold: DD + 40% slow + 40% snare (Cold -100). Targeted AE Fire: DD (Fire -100). Targeted AE Disease: 300/tick + AC -147 + 60% agro (Disease -100). Single Poison: DD (Poison -100). Spread so the cones do not hit the whole raid.

**Tallon's Balance** is a PBAE (-300 all resists, Unresistable, AoE 300, 60s recast).

**Avatar Power** is a PBAE (100 DD + Cancel Magic, Unresistable, AoE 60). It strips levitate. Recast it.

Hail the Planar Projection. It only answers the killer's raid or group, lasts 10 minutes, and can give up to 72 flags. You need Grand Librarian Maelin after [Lord Mithaniel Marr](/strategy/lord_mithaniel_marr) for that hail. Then hail Maelin in Plane of Knowledge.
