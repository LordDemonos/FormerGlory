Fire trial. [Plane of Time](/strategy/plane_of_time). 18 people. 1 hour for all five trials.

Wave 1: three **a flame mephit**. Each death spawns two **an inferno mephit**. Kill the small ones before the next big one. Big mephits Unmezzable. Sometimes rootable. Small mephits sometimes mez or root.

Wave 2: **Kazrok of Fire**. Dispel him. Slow him. Adds at **75%**, **50%**, **25%**. **a flame mephit** is mezzable. **an inferno mephit** is rootable.

He is **Slowable** (75% mit). AE Rampage. Flurry. Dual Wield. Hits 1150. Attack delay 16. 200k HP.

Dispel **Pyrokinetic Aura** (self, 75 DS).

**Black Pyre** is a PBAE (225/tick + 50% spell haste, Fire -150, 3 min recast).
