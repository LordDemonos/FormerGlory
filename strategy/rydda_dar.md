Northeast [Halls of Honor](/strategy/halls_of_honor_trials) trial. Hail **Trydan Faye**, say **we are ready**, and kill A Custodian of Marr. He spawns on the custodian's death. Be ready.

He is **Slowable** (82% mit). Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 2060. Attack delay 14. 500k HP. He does not see invis.

**Crystal Roar** is a PBAE (Fear to 52, 100% snare, 35% slow, AC -177 / -236 cloth, Magic -200, AoE 300, 60s recast).

**Screeching Bellow** is a proc (PBAE, 2500 DD + 700/tick + 3s stun, Magic -200, 36 disease counters, AoE 20). Strip the DoT.

Hail Trydan Faye after the kill. Success is white text: *An ethereal mist descends from the air and surrounds your very being. Your body begins to emanate with the power of Trydan Faye.*
