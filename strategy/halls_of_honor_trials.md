There are three Halls of Honor trials. You need all three flags to click the portal into [Temple of Marr](/strategy/temple_of_marr). Without them the portal says you lack the will to pass through safely.

<a href="/assets/img/HoH-Trials-Map.jpg" target="_blank">
  <img src="/assets/img/HoH-Trials-Map.jpg" alt="HoH-Trials-Map"/>
</a>

Access is **<a href="/strategy/aerin_dar">Aerin\`Dar</a>**. After that hail, drop the lair floor, clear the tunnel, and click the lightning at the end.

Each trial starts by saying **we are ready** to the trial NPC, which spawns A Custodian of Marr. Killing the custodian starts the event.

Hail the trial NPC after the win. Success is white text: *An ethereal mist descends from the air and surrounds your very being.* Trydan Faye, Rhaliq Trell, and Alekson Garn respawn in 3 days.

## Trial 1 — Trydan Faye

Northeast. Dragon trial.

![Trydan-Faye](/assets/img/Trydan-Faye.jpg)

![Custodian-of-Marr](/assets/img/Custodian-of-Marr.jpg)

Hail **Trydan Faye**, say **we are ready**. Kill the Custodian of Marr to spawn **<a href="/strategy/rydda_dar">Rydda\`Dar</a>**. Be ready. He spawns on the custodian's death.

He is **Slowable** (82% mit). Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 2060. Attack delay 14. 500k HP. He does not see invis.

**Crystal Roar** is a PBAE (Fear to 52, 100% snare, 35% slow, AC -177 / -236 cloth, Magic -200, AoE 300, 60s recast). **Screeching Bellow** is a proc (PBAE, 2500 DD + 700/tick + 3s stun, Magic -200, 36 disease counters, AoE 20). Strip the DoT.

Hail Trydan Faye after the kill.

## Trial 2 — Rhaliq Trell

Northwest. Three-room villager defense. There is no named raid boss. Waves plus protecting villagers in Room 1.

![Rhaliq-Trell](/assets/img/Rhaliq-Trell.jpg)

Hail **Rhaliq Trell**, say **we are ready**. Kill the custodian. Mobs spawn in each room. Split the raid or put most people in Room 1 and kite Rooms 2 and 3 until Room 1 is clear. You cannot harm the villagers. If mobs kill them, the trial fails.

Hail Rhaliq Trell in Room 1 when everything is dead.

## Trial 3 — Alekson Garn

Southeast. Three rooms, then a named in each room.

![Alekson-Garn](/assets/img/Alekson-Garn.jpg)

Hail **Alekson Garn**, say **we are ready**. Kill the custodian. About five to six mobs spawn per room. After a room's trash dies, that room's named spawns. All three named must die. Protect the NPCs in the rooms. If they die, the trial fails.

1. **[Advocent Joran](/strategy/advocent_joran)** — Room 1. **Slowable** (80% mit). Flurry. Dual Wield. Hits 1113. 115k HP.
2. **[Halgoz Rellinic](/strategy/halgoz_rellinic)** — Room 2. **Slowable** (60% mit). Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 1402. 200k HP.
3. **[Freegan Haun](/strategy/freegan_haun)** — Room 3. **Slowable** (60% mit). Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 1402. 200k HP.

Rooms can help each other once theirs is clear. Hail Alekson Garn in Room 1 when the last named is dead.
