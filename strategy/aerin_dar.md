[Plane of Valor](/strategy/plane_of_valor) raid. Click the orb in the prep room to port into the lair. The orb only works if he is up.

Pull him off the center platform. That floor opens after he dies. The Planar Projection spawns where he dies.

![Aerin-Dar-2](/assets/img/Aerin-Dar-2.png)

He is **Slowable**. Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 1347. 300k HP.

**Glass Shards** is a proc: 3000 DD + 45% slow, Magic -150.

**Glass Roar** is a 200-range PBAE: 700 DD, then 500/tick for 42 seconds, Magic -150, 36s recast. Fear up to level 52.

Eight **a mindless minion** (25k HP, hits 544, unmezzable) and **Rahlgon** (30k HP, hits 656, mezzable) start untargetable and become targetable during the fight. Root the minions. Mez Rahlgon. They can be rooted. Finish them after the dragon.

On death he spawns **A Planar Projection**. Hail it for the flag. The projection only answers the killer's raid or group, lasts 10 minutes, and can give up to 72 flags. You need a completed Justice trial for that hail to grant Halls of Honor. Then drop down the center floor, clear the tunnel, and click the lightning at the end to zone into [Halls of Honor](/strategy/halls_of_honor_trials).
