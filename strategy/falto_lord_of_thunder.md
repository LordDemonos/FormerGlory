Relv event in [Plane of Storms](/strategy/plane_of_storms). Hail **Relv the Mysterious** in the northwest forest. Follow his dialog through **we are courageous enough to go forth**. Group leaders say **we are ready** to port in.

Kill the mephits. Each death splits into three. When they are dead, he spawns. **3 hours**. He summons adds. Those adds do not split.

He is **Unslowable**. Rampage. Dual Wield. Corpse Camper. Hits 1137. 305k HP. Fire immune. **Maelstrom of Thunder** is a proc (Targeted AE, 900 DD, Magic 0, 12s recast).

After loot, say **I am ready** at the fallen treant.
