Phase 5. [Plane of Time](/strategy/plane_of_time). He does not leash. Kill him on the floor.

He is **Unslowable**. Rampage. Dual Wield. Hits 1600. Attack delay 14. Assist 1. 1.7M HP.

Emotes raise his melee as the fight goes. Cure **Black Plague**. Move out of **Rain of Bile**.

**Black Plague** is a PBAE (700/tick + 80% snare, Disease -300, 18 disease, 20s recast). Cure disease.

**Rain of Bile** is a Targeted AE (975 DD + 275 mana, Poison -300, 60s recast).
